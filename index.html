About the Science

Older adults with Stage G3a-A1 Chronic Kidney Disease (eGFR 45-59 mL/min/1.73m², no albuminuria) are often categorized as "low risk". However, our study reveals a hidden subgroup driven by Accelerated Biological Aging.

Using the Phenotypic Age algorithm (Levine et al.), this tool quantifies your biological age to stratify mortality risk, offering a more precise assessment than kidney function alone.
